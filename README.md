# AfriWell Bridge (AWB)

> **“Living out the Gospel by furthering Christ healing and teaching ministry to bring wholeness through healthcare and education to all men.”**  
> *Galatians 6:10*

AfriWell Bridge (AWB) is a Christian humanitarian and community development initiative established to expand access to essential healthcare and educational opportunities for vulnerable, underserved, and hard-to-reach communities in Africa.

---

## 🌟 Flagship Programs

1. **Rural Medical Outreach (“One Month of Healing and Hope”)**
   - Annual mobile healthcare clinics in underserved rural communities.
   - Free doctor consultations, vital screening (blood pressure, blood glucose, malaria), and health education.
   - Free essential prescription medicine dispensing.
   - Structured hospital referral pathways for surgical conditions (such as hernia repairs).

2. **Education Scholarship Program**
   - Annual scholarships for children from economically disadvantaged households.
   - Provision of school uniforms, tailored shoes, curriculum textbooks, backpacks, and stationery kits.
   - Academic retention support, one-on-one mentorship, and pathways to vocational/higher education.

Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your browser.
