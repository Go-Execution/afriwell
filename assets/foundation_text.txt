PROJECT BACKGROUND / RATIONALE
The AfriWell Bridge (AWB) is a proposed Christian humanitarian and community development organization established to serve vulnerable and underserved people in Africa through improved access to healthcare, education, compassion, and practical opportunities.
Across Africa, particularly in rural and hard-to-reach communities, poverty creates significant barriers to essential healthcare and education. Families may be unable to afford transportation, consultation, diagnostic services, medications, surgery, school fees, uniforms, books, or other basic needs.
During healthcare-sector experience as a hospital administrator, we encountered individuals and families living for years with medical conditions that could potentially have been addressed earlier if financial and access barriers had been reduced. Conditions such as hernia illustrate how people may experience prolonged suffering because they cannot afford transportation, consultation, medication, or surgical care. Through our experience dealing with different clients or patients, we learned the struggles of most people when it comes to the healthcare, they do not know what is available to them and even if they do know, the means of getting there is another challenge. 
Preventable and treatable illnesses, including malaria and other common conditions, also continue to place pressure on vulnerable families. Distance from health facilities, limited health information, and transportation challenges and poverty can contribute to delayed care.
Education faces similar challenges. Many children from low-income households struggle to remain in school because their families cannot afford books, school bags, uniforms, learning materials, tuition or other school-related expenses. Young people who complete secondary school may also have limited access to higher education, vocational training and employment opportunities.
The foundation therefore seeks to connect health, education, Christian compassion and community development by bringing practical assistance to communities where needs are greatest.








PROBLEM STATEMENT
Vulnerable communities in Africa face interconnected barriers:
Healthcare
Limited financial resources for healthcare; 
Long distances to health facilities; 
Transportation difficulties; 
Delayed treatment-seeking; 
Limited diagnostic and specialist services; 
Inability to purchase prescribed medicines; 
Limited access to surgical services; and 
Insufficient preventive health information. 
Education
Inability to meet school-related costs; 
Lack of books and learning materials; 
Lack of school bags and uniforms; 
Irregular attendance and school dropout; 
Limited access to secondary and tertiary education; and 
Limited skills and employment opportunities for young people. 
These challenges can contribute to preventable suffering, reduced educational attainment and limited economic opportunities.
OVERALL GOAL
To improve the health, educational opportunities, dignity and long-term wellbeing of vulnerable individuals and communities in Africa by reducing financial and access barriers to essential healthcare and education.
SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
The foundation will seek to:
Provide free or subsidized healthcare services to underserved rural communities. 
Conduct an annual medical outreach in selected rural communities. 
Mobilize qualified healthcare professionals for humanitarian medical missions. 
Promote disease prevention, early detection and health education. 
Facilitate referrals for patients requiring services beyond outreach capacity. 
Provide scholarships and educational assistance to vulnerable children. 
Provide selected students with books, school bags and essential learning materials. 
Promote school retention and continued education. 
Support opportunities for higher education, vocational training and skills development. 
Develop sustainable partnerships with health, education, faith-based and community institutions.

The proposed project is expected to produce the following outputs:
Output 1: Underserved rural communities identified and reached through community-based health interventions.
Output 2: Vulnerable individuals receive free or subsidized consultations, screening, treatment and health education.
Output 3: Patients requiring additional care are identified and referred where appropriate.
Output 4: Vulnerable children receive scholarships and/or essential school supplies.
Output 5: Community members receive information on disease prevention, healthy practices and appropriate health-seeking behavior.
Output 6: Partnerships established with healthcare institutions, schools, churches, professionals and community organizations.
Output 7: A monitoring and reporting system established to measure program reach and outcomes.

FOUNDATION MISSION
Our mission is to expand access to quality healthcare and education for underserved communities, particularly in rural West Africa, through compassionate service, preventive health outreach, scholarships, and educational support. We are also committed to promoting critical thinking, encouraging people to become independent, and believe that healthcare and education belong to all. Through healthcare, education, community empowerment, and awareness, we empower individuals and communities to make informed decisions, embrace positive change, and build healthier, more enlightened, and sustainable futures.
