AFRIWELL BRIDGE — LOGO PACKAGE (Concept 1a)
=============================================

FOLDERS
01_Primary-Lockup   Full logo (icon + AFRiWELL BRIDGE). Use wherever space allows.
02_Icon-Mark        Africa emblem only. Use for small spaces, stamps, watermarks.
03_Platforms        Ready-to-upload files sized for Web, iOS, Android, Social Media, Email.

THEMES
Light-Theme   Navy logo for white / light backgrounds.
Dark-Theme    Cream + gold logo for navy / dark backgrounds.

BACKGROUNDS
Transparent-Background   No background (PNG / SVG). Place on any matching surface.
Solid-Background         Logo on its brand background colour (PNG / JPG / SVG).

FORMATS
SVG   Vector — infinitely scalable. Use for print, signage, website, and sending to printers/designers.
PNG   Raster with transparency support. Use for documents, presentations, web.
JPG   Raster, solid background only. Use where PNG is not accepted.
ICO   Website favicon (03_Platforms/Web).

BRAND COLOURS
Navy          #13284F   RGB 19 40 79
Gold          #B8902F   RGB 184 144 47    (on light backgrounds)
Light Gold    #D9B25A   RGB 217 178 90    (on dark backgrounds)
Cream         #F6F4F0   RGB 246 244 240   (logo colour on dark)
White         #FFFFFF

TYPEFACE
Archivo Black/ExtraBold (Google Fonts, free). Text in all files is converted to outlines.

PLATFORM NOTES
Web       Upload favicon.ico, favicon.svg (auto light/dark), apple-touch-icon.png and site.webmanifest to your site root.
iOS       AppIcon-1024x1024 is the App Store icon (no transparency, as Apple requires). iOS rounds the corners automatically.
Android   Copy mipmap-* folders into app/src/main/res. Adaptive icon foreground + background provided.
Social    Profile pictures are safe for circular crops. Banners keep the logo inside each platform's safe area.

USAGE
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "B" in BRIDGE.
- Do not stretch, recolour, rotate or add effects.
- Minimum size: lockup 120px wide on screen / 30mm in print; icon 16px / 8mm.
