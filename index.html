The Reality

Bridging the Distance to Care & Learning

In rural African communities, poverty creates severe barriers to the two essentials every human deserves: health and education.

Families endure treatable conditions like hernia or malaria for years simply because transportation and hospital fees are unaffordable. Meanwhile, bright children are forced out of school when their families cannot afford uniforms, books, or bags.

AfriWell Bridge (AWB) steps directly into that gap—mobilizing healthcare professionals and providing education supplies with Christian compassion.

Focused Interventions

Our Two Flagship Programs

Practical, high-impact solutions addressing the root barriers in underserved rural communities.

Annual 1-Month Mission

Rural Medical Outreach

We bring doctors, nurses, and pharmacists directly to isolated villages to provide free medical consultations, screening, and treatment.

  • Free doctor consultations & general medical triage
  • Blood pressure, glucose & malaria testing
  • Free prescription medication dispensing
  • Referral network for hernia & surgical hospital care

Annual Academic Cycle

Education Scholarships

We remove every financial barrier preventing children from attending school, fostering independence and critical thinking.

  • Tuition support & school-related fee coverage
  • Tailored school uniforms & durable shoes
  • Backpacks, curriculum textbooks & stationery kits
  • One-on-one student mentorship & guidance
