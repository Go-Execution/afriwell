Frontline Healthcare Rationale

Why AfriWell Bridge Was Established

During healthcare-sector experience as a hospital administrator in West Africa, the Founder encountered hundreds of individuals and families living for years with treatable conditions like hernia, cataracts, and chronic infections simply because they could not afford bus fare, consultation fees, or surgery.

Education faces the same devastating barrier. Bright children drop out of primary and secondary school not because of lack of ability, but because parents cannot afford simple school uniforms, shoes, exercise books, or exam fees.

Executive Leadership & Founder’s Story

Cyrus N. Younquoi

Founder & Executive Director • Hospital Administrator

Born in Karnwee Town, Nimba County, Republic of Liberia, Cyrus’s early education was repeatedly displaced by Liberia’s civil war and economic hardship. Through sheer perseverance, family sacrifices, and a life-changing scholarship championed by Dr. Julie Taylor, he graduated with high honors, earned a B.B.A. in Human Resource Management, an MSc in Healthcare Organization Management in Türkiye, and is currently a PhD Candidate in Public Health (Environmental Health) at ACE-PUTOR.

Having served as a classroom teacher, Assistant Admissions Advisor, Data Analyst at Refuge Place International (RPI), and Hospital Administrator at Esther & Jereline Medical Center in Ganta, Cyrus founded AfriWell Bridge so no rural child or patient is left behind due to poverty.

Founder & Executive Director

Cyrus N. Younquoi

From Humble Beginnings to a Life of Purpose, Education, and Service

Early Roots & Resilience: Born on January 17, 1994, in Karnwee Town, Lessonnoh Chiefdom, Nimba County, Republic of Liberia, to late parents Mr. Moses Younquoi and Mrs. Martha Borman Younquoi, Cyrus grew up as the youngest of four children shaped by village life, warmth of family, and deep determination.

Perseverance Through War & Displacement: Starting school at Vokpor High School and later Saclepea Red Cross High School with cousin Mr. Roosevelt Zarwee and Mrs. Dee Zarwee, Cyrus’s education was repeatedly interrupted by Liberia’s civil war. Supported through displacement by family—including Kpallah Inland Mission, his late uncle Mr. Robert Yeanay in Tiapa, and late aunt Mother Theresa Daysee in Saclepea—he attended Johnny Voker High School, J. W. Pearson High School in Ganta City, and night school at New Destiny Christian High School in Monrovia, graduating with high honors.

Higher Education & Global Mentorship: In 2014, Cyrus entered St. Clements University, graduating with high honors in 2021 with a B.B.A. in Human Resource Management. Through a life-changing scholarship—and the invaluable mentorship and unwavering belief of Dr. Julie Taylor—he traveled to Türkiye to earn an MSc in Healthcare Organization Management with high honors at Cyprus International University. He also earned an Advanced Diploma in Entrepreneurship, Leadership, and Management (Tazama Leadership School of Pennsylvania, USA, 2022) and a Certificate in Customer Relationship Management (Kingston Business School, Manchester, UK). Today, he is a PhD Candidate in Public Health (Environmental Health) at the University of Port Harcourt’s World Bank Africa Centre of Excellence (ACE-PUTOR).

Service, Healthcare Administration & Leadership: Cyrus’s career spans classroom teaching at Betty Memorial Institute (Grand Cape Mount County, 2008–2014), serving as Assistant Admissions Advisor at Tazama Leadership School (2022–2024), Data Analyst at Refuge Place International (RPI), and serving as Hospital Administrator at Esther & Jereline Medical Center in Ganta—the second largest government hospital in Nimba County, Liberia—alongside founding Blue Lake Group of Companies.

“I began my journey with very little, but I carried something powerful with me: hope. Circumstances may delay you, but they do not have to define you. Education can open doors that once seemed impossible to reach, and every difficult chapter can become preparation for a greater purpose.”
