Frontline Healthcare Rationale

Why AfriWell Bridge Was Established

During years of healthcare-sector experience as hospital administrators in West Africa, our founders encountered hundreds of individuals and families living for years with treatable conditions like hernia, cataracts, and chronic infections simply because they could not afford bus fare, consultation fees, or surgery.

Education faces the same devastating barrier. Bright children drop out of primary and secondary school not because of lack of ability, but because parents cannot afford simple school uniforms, shoes, exercise books, or exam fees.

Our Core Declaration

“Living out the Gospel by furthering Christ healing and teaching ministry to bring wholeness through healthcare and education to all men.”

Grassroots Commitment

Reaching the last mile in rural counties where medical clinics and learning materials are scarce.
